# Stateshift

Treat this as the only established set of facts. If a field is empty, it is unknown: say so, do not guess.

- **What it does:** Developer relations and go-to-market consulting for developer-first companies, founded by Jono Bacon. Turns developer interest into adoption, retention, and revenue.
- **Positioning and differentiators:** Embeds with the client's team instead of delivering a strategy deck and leaving. Maps audience personas (economic buyer, internal champion, technical user), defines the few metrics that tie to business outcomes, and installs repeatable frameworks the team can run without Stateshift. Works with about 20 companies at a time. Stance: no sales theater, no vanity metrics.
- **Voice and tone:** Direct, practical, developer-literate, grounded in ecosystem architecture; clear, precise, devoid of corporate fluff or generic marketing jargon.
- **Topics to avoid or handle carefully:** Do not recommend or endorse surface-level growth hacks, vanity metrics (e.g., page views, total sign-ups), or generic marketing templates. Critiquing them is fine and on-brand. Do not pitch or criticize traditional agency retainers.

## Visual style

Dark and cinematic. Near-black navy backgrounds (approx. #0B0A1F) with soft violet glow (approx. #7C5CFF), deep indigo for label chips (approx. #3A1FB5), lavender for emphasis words (approx. #B9A9FF), white for primary text. Headlines are large, bold, geometric sans-serif in white, often all caps for stat graphics, with the key phrase in lavender. Small tracked-out uppercase labels (e.g. "DEEP DIVE", "FEATURING") sit in a slanted parallelogram chip or as plain small text. Logo: white uppercase wide-spaced STATESHIFT wordmark, top right. Video and event graphics use a dark, blurred photo with a dark overlay and semi-transparent dark boxes behind the headline, plus a circular headshot with a thin colored ring and name/title. Stat and explainer graphics use a subtle dot texture, simple flat people or object icons in gray, with the focal element glowing violet (e.g. a lit doorway with the Stateshift "S"), and a thin arrow line as the call to action. Mood: moody, premium, developer-native, one idea per graphic. Formats seen: 16:9 for video thumbnails, roughly 4:5 portrait for feed graphics.
